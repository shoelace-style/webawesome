Start with a <a href="/docs/theming-overview">theme</a>. A theme, a color palette, and variant colors stack into the overall look, and you mix and match them with classes on your <code>&lt;html&gt;</code> element. To go further, override Web Awesome's <code>--wa-</code> design tokens in your own stylesheet. No preprocessor required. For one component at a time, <a href="/docs/customizing">Customizing &amp; Theming</a> covers CSS parts, custom properties, and custom states.
