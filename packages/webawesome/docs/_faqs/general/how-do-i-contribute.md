question: How can I contribute to Web Awesome?
order: 40
synonyms: [contribute, pull request, open source, community, issues]

---
There are plenty of ways to contribute, and most of them aren't code: a well-written bug report, a fix to the documentation, triaging issues, answering someone's question in <a href="/support#discord">the community chat</a>. If you do want to send code, open an issue first (unless it's something small like a typo) and point your pull request at the `next` branch. The <a href="/docs/resources/contributing">contributing guide</a> covers the rest, including what makes a bug report easy to act on.
