question: Is Web Awesome accessible?
order: 90
synonyms: [a11y, screen reader, aria, keyboard, compliance]

---
Accessibility isn't binary, so there's no honest yes-or-no here. What we can commit to: everything we build is developed with accessibility in mind, and we test and improve every component as best we can. But accessible building blocks won't make the rest of your page compliant on their own. Our <a href="/docs/resources/accessibility">Accessibility Commitment</a> explains the whole position.
