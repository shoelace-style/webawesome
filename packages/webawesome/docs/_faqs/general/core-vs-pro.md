Core is the free, open source library, and it's the whole foundation: components, themes, color palettes, native styles, and CSS utilities. Pro builds on top of it with:

- Pro components, themes, and color palettes
- The Theme Builder and color tools
- Design patterns and the Figma Design Kit
- A perpetual license and support from our team

The <a href="/pro#comparison">side-by-side comparison</a> covers every row.
